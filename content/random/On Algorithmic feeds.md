Description: On how algorithmic feeds feeds themselves on humans
---

The other day I was watching the `tsoding` stream as usual and he talked about something that left me thinking for quite a long time. 

The idea he was discussing is how algorithmic feeds are designed in such a way that do not let us go. Like You know your feed sucks and you no longer want to shape your understanding with what you are presented with on the screen by these big tech giants. You want independent thinking and viewing. Let me guess over 90% of the content I watch is basically useless to me in day to day life but I watch it regardless due to curiosity. I am letting algorithm shape as a human, evolving my understanding. If they want they will make you conservative by deliberately showing videos that profits them. 

The solution is simple, just close the feed option. Many platform allow us to do that but who does it? No one. At least I haven't met anyone yet that does this. The solution is there it is simple but it is the implementation that is hard. These feeds are deliberately made so that you can not disable them, whenever you feel like doing it, it reminds you that though most things/videos shown to you is crap, there might be a gem of a content waiting for you to discover it. It is this greed of finding new content that we like that keeps us from disabling the feeds and I could not agree more.
